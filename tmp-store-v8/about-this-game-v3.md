## Short description (unchanged unless you want it changed)

## About this game (English, Steam BBCode)

```
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/about-01-radar-round.webm[/img]

Esports Manager: FPS puts you in charge of a fictional tactical-FPS club. You start near the bottom of the world rankings and decide everything between you and a trophy: who plays, what they buy, which plan they run and when to stop and regroup.

[h2]Watch the round, not a dice roll[/h2]
Every round plays out on a live tactical radar. Players walk real routes across the map, hold angles and only fight what they can actually see. Smokes block sight, flashes blind, defenders rotate when they get information, and after a plant they regroup and retake. Trades and clutches happen because of where your players stand, and the round feed tells you who won each duel and where.

[h2]Write the playbook[/h2]
Give your team a named call for every map and every buy: a default, a fast execute onto one site, a split, a fake or a rush on attack, and a stack or an aggressive setup on defence. Run the same call too often and opponents start reading it. Share your playbook with a code, and see over the season which plans actually win you rounds.
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/about-02-playbook.webm[/img]

[h2]Call it live[/h2]
Between rounds you make the buy: save, force, half, full or a double AWP setup. When the team drops rounds in a row, burn a tactical timeout and steady them.
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/about-03-call-the-buy.webm[/img]

[h2]Know your odds[/h2]
Ratings reflect what a player actually brings to the server, so a stronger team on paper is a stronger team in the match. Before every match you see your win chance, and it moves as you prepare: counter their style, anti-strat their star, pay for a VOD review.

[h2]Draft Cup[/h2]
Want a quick run instead of a full career? Draft a five from a random pool on a fixed budget, one player per role, and take them through a knockout cup. Every draft is different.

[h2]Sign people, not numbers[/h2]
Every player has a personality as well as ratings. Will they mentor the rookie, clash with the captain or fold under pressure? Build a roster around roles that fit together: entry, AWPer, rifler, support and in-game leader. Form, morale and chemistry move every week, and the game shows you why.

[h2]Run the whole club[/h2]
Negotiate transfers and contracts with real clauses, balance sponsors and salaries, hire staff, invest in facilities and keep the board's targets in sight. Every new career starts in a freshly shuffled world, so no two seasons play out the same.
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/about-04-victory.webm[/img]
```
