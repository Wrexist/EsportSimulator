# Glossary

The words every screen uses for the game's core ideas, per language, so the
same thing is never called two names. Translators and reviewers: change a term
here first, then in the files.

General rules for every language:

- Counter-Strike terms stay in the form players use in that language's
  community: AWP, AWPer, IGL, entry, lurker, rifler, clutch, eco, force buy,
  half buy, full buy, ace, retake, execute, rotation, utility, frag, MVP,
  Major. Most stay English; the table gives the local form where one is common.
- Map names, team names, player nicknames, tournament names and "Draft Cup"
  stay as they are.
- OVR, K/D, ADR, KAST and rating stay as abbreviations.
- Address the player informally where the language allows it in games
  (German "du", French "tu", Spanish "tú", Portuguese "você", Russian "ты",
  Polish "ty"), matching how other sports management games speak.
- Money stays in the game's own format ($1.2M).
- Players can be anyone: avoid past tenses and adjectives that mark the
  player's gender (Polish "zagrałeś", Russian "ты играл", "готов"); rephrase
  around them instead.
