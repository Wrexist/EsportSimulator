★Coming next: Seasons & Careers

The next update is about long careers: players who age like real pros, a map pool that changes each season, and harder choices about who plays when the schedule gets heavy.

  • Honest odds. Results are judged against the odds. A shock win lifts morale and form more than a routine one, and the result screen tells you how likely the result was.
  • Workload and knocks. Every player shows a load and injury-risk rating. When someone is running hot you decide: rest them or play them. A minor knock can be played through, at a cost.
  • Seasonal map pool. Each new season one map is benched and the veto adapts. Prepare for what is in the pool, not what was.
  • Age curve. Young players grow fastest, peaks arrive in the mid twenties, and decline starts around thirty. In-game leaders and AWPers last longer.
  • Full costs. Staff, transfers, academy promotions and facility upgrades show the total over the deal, not just this week.
  • Trophy collection. Titles now add rings, medals and replica items to a collection on the trophies screen.
  • Sandbox saves. Turn any save into a sandbox and edit money, players, contracts and teams. Sandbox saves are marked and do not count for achievements.
Devlogs for each of these come out on Steam news in the week before the update.
