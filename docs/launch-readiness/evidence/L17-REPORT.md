## Earlier implementation pass

- **AI finances:** `engine/recruitment.ts` and `engine/ai/infrastructure.ts` evaluate the projected club's continuing income, wages, facilities, academy and role-training costs before optional staff/build/upgrade spending. Optional investment must have nonnegative recurring net after the change and sufficient purchase cash. Distressed clubs can still seek sponsors under the existing ranking/tier restrictions. No cash, prize income or sponsorship payout was invented to pass the audit.
- **Registration:** the manual commit now calls the entry-rule check instead of relying on the UI. Manual and automatic registration require five distinct, living senior players with current club contracts and no competing owner. Academy-held players cannot satisfy this check. Explicit past tournament instances are rejected. This validates current registration; future roster locks, contract expiry at match time and every tournament format remain separate acceptance work.
- **Match participation:** `store/slices/match-simulation-slice.ts` previously sanitized the entire roster into result rows, manufacturing substitute participation. It now uses the same first-five lineup convention as the legacy match simulator. Unused substitutes no longer get result rows, XP, fatigue, appearances or contractual win bonuses from this commit path. The regression supplies an extra bench record deliberately. No historical completed results were rewritten.
- **Academy scheduling:** only seven integer weekday slots and unlocked drill IDs are accepted. Weekly processing also ignores invalid/locked legacy schedule entries, executes in weekday order and applies energy consumption/recovery between sessions. Fatigue therefore affects later sessions. The displayed energy delta reflects actual bounded energy, and stat reports reflect applied changes. A report for the current week prevents a second academy development/scouting update.
- **Development:** fractional academy improvements persist instead of being rounded away each week. Both academy and weekly senior training preserve specialist attributes already above potential. Retired players no longer receive weekly team training. Role training rejects foreign/retired players, unknown roles and a player's existing primary role.
- **Ownership:** discarding a pending prospect cannot delete an arbitrary rostered/contracted/enrolled player. Promotion rejects retired or conflicting ownership/contracts. Existing academy cleanup and contract creation remain in place.
- **Team drills and UI:** active matches block drills; role trainees and retired players sit them out; an empty available roster cannot consume a slot. Recovery remains usable above the exhaustion threshold. Non-finite effects are rejected. Training copy explains immediate slot/effect timing and potential caps; the unsupported coach-level percentage was removed. Academy copy explains daily order and the below-15-energy XP penalty. These changes extend the existing UI, not a parallel training system.
