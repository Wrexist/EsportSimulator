# L20 - Careers, board expectations, jobs and season outcomes

Reviewed 14 September 2026. **Partial: local implementation and controlled career validation pass; full launch acceptance remains open.** Dependencies L16-L19 retain their unresolved world/economy/UI/replay work.

## Changes

- Store and engine job acceptance now use one authoritative action. Accepted, declined, withdrawn, expired, wrong-tenure and same-club offers cannot be reused. Reading an offer does not consume it. Active matches and ended careers block acceptance; the existing 12-week job cooldown remains. Negotiation and decline share availability checks, preventing repeated loyalty rewards. Accepted moves withdraw the other offers and unresolved previous-club choices.
- Signing funds are finite, bounded, and ledgered once using the offer ID. Existing design is retained: four times the negotiated weekly offer goes to the new club as a one-time payment. The advertised weekly manager salary is not a recurring club income stream. Weekly offers now avoid ended careers, the move cooldown and concurrent open offers; offer generation no longer sorts the world team array in place.
- Club academy enrollment, training schedules, reports, scouting missions/results, watchlists, sponsor offers and board state stay with their club. `TeamSaveData.managementState` stores the inactive club's management data; returning restores it. New clubs receive empty management plans and a fresh board unless previously managed. Pending celebrations/legend selection/recap do not transfer. Archived academy players remain reserved against recruitment, their records are protected from garbage collection and archived academy upkeep is charged. Restoration filters retired academy enrollments.
- Career summaries now record the departing stint and subsequent stints separately; one season can contain multiple managed clubs. Aggregates count unique seasons rather than stint rows. New `tenureStartWeek` cuts off the incoming club's pre-arrival record. Historical attribution uses whole weeks: the departure week belongs to the old stint, and incoming history starts the following week. Club selection changes immediately; finer same-week/day result attribution remains open.
- Prize totals use the PRIZE ledger category rather than all club income. New summaries carry optional `prizeMoney`; unknown historical prize amounts are not reconstructed from ordinary revenue. Fresh peak-ranking tracking no longer starts at an invented world #30.
- Manager legacy merges trophies earned during recorded stints and the current tenure, with season/event deduplication. Joining an established club no longer inherits trophies won before arrival. Previously achieved profile peaks remain monotonic.
- New boards for lower-ranked clubs receive a persisted relative ranking target (10% improvement when outside the top 30, bounded by the tier's existing target) rather than automatically demanding a top-26/top-14 finish from a club around #175. The dashboard shows the persisted target. A stint under 13 weeks receives a grace review without cash, confidence or reputation changes. Quarterly form and trophy checks exclude pre-tenure results. Reviews report target and actual finish; notice is still required before sacking. Repeated board payment IDs cannot pay twice, and debt cannot produce a negative backing bonus.
- Three contextual decision templates cover low confidence, high fatigue and three-win momentum. They offer visible paid/free tradeoffs using real morale, loyalty, fatigue and chemistry effects. One unresolved decision per club, an eight-week cooldown and two-week deadlines limit repetition. Resolution checks ownership, availability and affordability before applying bounded effects; repeat clicks are inert.
- The season recap previously counted every world match as a managed loss unless the player team won it. It now uses saved managed stints, or a team-filtered historical fallback, and the selected season's fixed dates. It shows managed weeks and recorded financial change. The previous lifetime-rating MVP guess is no longer presented as a season MVP; a proper season-specific spotlight remains to be implemented.

## Save compatibility and rollback

No schema bump. Optional `TeamSaveData.managementState`, `ManagerDetails.tenureStartWeek`, and `SeasonSummary.prizeMoney` extend existing objects that the canonical snapshot already persists. The archive round-trip regression uses normal SaveManager persistence. New boards optionally store `rankTarget`; existing boards retain their prior targets until reviewed. Old summary rows are not rewritten and previously awarded profile peaks are not reduced.

Older builds do not understand archived club-management ownership. Do not use a code reset as a save migration: retain an exported career and design a preservation-first migration before loading a newly switched career in an older build. The workspace includes extensive earlier uncommitted work; rollback only the reviewed L20 hunks. Source/build/fixture identity and untouched Mirage hashes are in the identity artifact.
