# L22 first-session observation protocol

Status: ready to run; no participants tested. This protocol and blank worksheet are preparation, not usability evidence.

## Cohort and environment

Recruit 12 people who have not played this game. Record only anonymous participant IDs and their self-reported management-game familiarity. Do not substitute developers, automated agents or repeated sessions for fresh participants. Use a separate Windows test profile per participant, with the same candidate build and no owner saves. Record build ID, resolution, input method and initial club. Confirm participation and any optional recording separately; do not collect unrelated personal data.

Start on Main Menu. Use the ordinary New Career flow, with career club eligibility enabled and the first-session guide preference on. Club choice is part of the test: let participants choose an eligible club and record its starting cash and available starters. An unfinished setup reload is a separate recovery exercise after the primary session. Do not advance an owner career or delete an owner draft.

## Standard instruction, read once

?Start a career. Assess the team and what you can afford. Make one deliberate weekly training/activity decision, complete your first match, and use the report to identify something you would change next. You can skip the guide if you prefer. Tell us what you are trying to do as you play.?

Allow 30 minutes for the primary objective. This limit is a proposed test condition, not a measured time-to-fun claim. Wins are not required. Default training is a valid deliberate choice if the participant can explain it. If the chosen club lacks five available players, affordable recruitment and correct starter selection are part of reaching the match.

## Observer rules

Do not point to controls, explain eligibility, suggest a plan, choose a club or recover the session during the primary attempt. Answer ?Please use what the game tells you? for navigation questions. Record requests for help and confusion verbatim where possible. Any facilitator instruction that enables progress is a rescue and fails the no-rescue criterion. A crash, lost setup, inaccessible control or save failure is a product failure; retain its evidence instead of excluding the participant. Participant withdrawal is recorded as incomplete, never counted as success.

Time from Main Menu to club creation, first meaningful decision, first match and result review. A meaningful decision is a deliberate plan/recruitment choice explained in terms of cost, training, energy or roster need, not opening a link or dismissing text. Record wrong turns, ineffective clicks, abandoned controls, restarts, rescue, completion and one chosen follow-up.

## After the primary attempt

Ask: ?What does your cash balance cover??, ?What will your chosen plan change, and when??, ?Why did you choose your next action?? Do not reveal answers until observations are logged. Run separate guided checks for skip/replay, save/reload midway, loading another career, custom-club draft recovery, changing sandbox eligibility, insufficient cash and no scheduled match. These assisted checks do not count toward the primary success rate.

## Decision rule

L22.A1 requires at least 10 of 12 fresh participants to complete the defined objective without facilitator rescue. Report numerator and denominator, withdrawals/failures and the distribution of decision times; do not report only the average. List the three most common confusions and fix blockers before repeating with a new cohort. The other L22 acceptance criteria still require real reachable-control/skip/replay and recurring-dashboard validation. A passing cohort does not clear L14, packaged Windows or content gates.

Use L22-player-sessions.csv for results. All 12 rows currently say NOT_RUN. Keep exact build identity and screenshots/logs alongside observed failures.
