## Implementation

- Replaced the live timeout's flat +6 percentage-point win bonus and opponent-momentum suppression with regrouping: for the next two generated rounds, subtract at most two losses from the managed team's tilt calculation. Actual loss bonuses, economy and opponent momentum are unchanged. This is an aggregate pressure mechanic, not spatial coordination. Two timeouts per series, strategy breaks only; an active timeout cannot stack. The click handler commits charge refs immediately, preventing double clicks from spending twice. Invalid saved counters reject resume. Visible text explains timing and duration.
- Applied paid mental preparation to the correct team in live round strength calculation, matching the existing instant engine behavior. Legacy preparation without a team ID retains its home-team interpretation.
- Custom buy loadouts now reach only the managed team in both live and instant match buying. Opponents use their own default buying logic. Explicit slot IDs take precedence over the order of a saved loadout array. Removed the dashboard's hidden quick-sim strength penalty; paid preparation still affects the team and live intervention remains available only during play.
- Generic fixture updates can no longer set or clear paid preparation flags. Purchase and veto actions reject foreign, finished, active/recoverable and past-date fixtures. Completed vetoes require the full BO1/3/5 pool; shortening the pool unseals it, and changing maps removes obsolete starting-side entries. Costs remain $2,500 for VOD review and $5,000 for mental reset, with one ledger debit per purchase. Generic mental reset cannot repeat within a week after a reset session. Zero morale now receives +15 rather than being treated as missing and becoming 85.
- Live preparation and quick simulation share starter resolution: distinct IDs, selected order, existing players and no retired starters. A missing or retired selected player is not silently replaced by a bench player. Instant simulation clones players and staff before talent adjustments, avoiding writes to the store outside its transaction. Active/recoverable matches block quick simulation instead of allowing a replacement result.
- New engine results and player-match commits record team-to-player lineup IDs. The report's overview, comparative statistics and economy grouping use those match-time IDs, so subsequent transfers do not move historical rows. Older results explicitly disclose their current-roster fallback. A compact own-lineup follow-up uses recorded opening duels or ADR and links to training, scouting, squad roles and the schedule. These are review suggestions, not claims that a statistic caused the loss.
