# OVR that predicts match results (2026-10-24)

## The problem

The shown OVR and the match engine measured different things.

- OVR was `0.5 × base skill + 0.35 × role fit + prestige bonus (up to +25)`, times an experience factor. Hidden `skill` was only 10% of base skill.
- The engine aims with `AIM_PER_SKILL × skill` plus small clamped weapon adjustments (`engine/match/round-sim/profile.ts`). `skill` decides the gunfights.
- In the snapshot, hidden skill is almost flat across tiers (elite clubs average 46, semi-pro 45, academy 44). The OVR spread between famous and unknown clubs came almost entirely from the prestige bonus, which the engine never sees.

## The fix

1. **OVR is the engine's strength** (`engine/match-rating.ts`). In skill points: `skill` + the role weapon's aim adjustment (the AWPer's AWP, everyone else's rifle, with the engine's clamps and weapon mastery) at 0.8 of its duel value + trading and the situational attributes at their measured weights (capped at ±6). A constant `OVR_SCALE_OFFSET` (6) keeps a new world's median OVR at the old 58, so tier thresholds, wages and the price curve keep their meaning. Reputation and experience no longer move OVR; they still raise market value.
2. **Reputation becomes real skill once.** The old prestige bonus (`(proScore/100)^1.5 × 25`) is added to `skill` and `potential` when a new career is created (`data/snapshot-loader.ts`) and by save migration v11 for existing careers (legends excluded). The world keeps its order, and the engine now plays it.

The match engine itself is unchanged (no tuning constant moved). The pro-benchmark audit (`scripts/audit-sight-engine.ts`) plays even 70-rated fixtures, so its results are unaffected.

## What each attribute is worth in the engine

`npx tsx scripts/ratings/ovr-agreement.ts exchange --attr <a> --delta 15 --samples 1200`: one attribute +15 for all five starters of the stronger of two evenly matched clubs, 1,200 paired maps (same seeds with and without).

|---|---|---|---|

| trading | +2.2 | +0.56 | 0.13 |

Role checks (2,000 paired maps, +25): rifle on the four non-AWPers +1.50 round share (0.27 skill points per rifle point, against 0.29 in a single duel); AWP on the AWPer +0.03 (snapshot AWPers already sit near the engine's +3.5 cap); skill on the AWPer alone +2.14 (an AWPer's skill is worth about 1.5 times an average player's).

## Does OVR predict results now?

`npx tsx scripts/ratings/ovr-agreement.ts maps --samples 6000 --seed 1` (4 shards), then `report`. Fresh snapshot career, AI path, BO1. Opponents: 50% within 25 places of the OVR table, 30% anyone, 20% a top-10 club against a club ranked 80–130. Team OVR = mean OVR of the starting five, as the squad and rankings pages show it.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Maps | 5,919 | 5,919 |
| Higher-OVR club wins | 53.9% | **62.5%** |
| OVR gap 0.5–2 | 53.6% | 53.6% |
| OVR gap 2–5 | 52.9% | 56.8% |
| OVR gap 5–10 | 55.0% | 58.8% |
| OVR gap 10–20 | 53.2% | 68.1% |
| OVR gap 20+ | 55.0% | **79.3%** |
| Top 10 vs ranked 80–130 (1,140 maps) | 53.9% | **74.4%** |
| Log-odds per OVR point (fit) | 0.011 | 0.054 |
| Log loss (coin flip 0.693) | 0.691 | 0.647 |
| Correlation, player OVR vs hidden skill | 0.45 | 0.99 (vs engine duel rating) |
| Correlation, club OVR vs club skill | 0.34 | 0.99 (vs club duel rating) |

0.054 log-odds per OVR point is the engine's own slope (win-odds `rating` 0.19 per duel point × 0.28 duel points per skill point = 0.053), so OVR now carries everything the players bring. The rest is the round dice, day form and team edge.

How steep this is (a 10-OVR edge ≈ 63% of maps, 20 ≈ 75%) is the engine's sensitivity to skill (`AIM_PER_SKILL`, `DUEL_SCALE`), a separate tuning call that would move the pro-benchmark calibration.

## Win chance still checks out

The tactics-page win chance (`engine/match/win-odds.ts`) was fitted on the flat world. On the new world (`scripts/odds/win-odds-calibration.ts generate --samples 4400 --seed 2`, 4,315 maps): mean error 1.07 points per 10% bucket (was 0.9), and a re-fit gives rating 0.20 / edge 2.8 against the shipped 0.19 / 2.5, within noise. `WIN_ODDS_FIT` is unchanged.

## Re-run

- Agreement: `npx tsx scripts/ratings/ovr-agreement.ts maps ...` then `report` (about 40 ms per map).
- Weights: `exchange --attr <name>`; update `MATCH_RATING_WEIGHTS` when match tuning changes.
- Test: `__tests__/match-rating.test.ts`.
