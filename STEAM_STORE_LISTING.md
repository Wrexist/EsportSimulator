## About this game

Esports Manager: FPS puts you in charge of a fictional esports club. You choose the players, shape their preparation and make the decisions behind the results. Build a team you believe in, then keep it competitive as the season unfolds.

### Build Your Squad

Find the players who fit your plan. Scout prospects, compare roles and attributes, recruit free agents and negotiate transfers. Balance immediate upgrades against the cost of keeping a competitive roster.

### Run The Club

Every signing leaves a mark on your budget. Negotiate salaries and contract lengths, manage sponsors, hire staff and invest in facilities and equipment. Decide where the next dollar will make the biggest difference.

### Shape Your Season

Set training priorities, prepare your lineup and choose your match approach. Follow the competition calendar, review results and track your place in the rankings. Respond to board expectations as your managerial career develops.

### Your club, your decisions

- Scout players and build a roster around complementary roles.
- Negotiate transfers, free-agent signings and player contracts.
- Plan training and develop prospects through your academy.
- Manage club finances, sponsors, staff, facilities and equipment.
- Prepare lineups and tactics, then review match statistics.
- Navigate tournaments, qualification, rankings and board expectations.
